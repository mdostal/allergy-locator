# My story — the chart with receipts

*The worked example behind this tool is my own allergy history. Identity stripped; the numbers
are real, straight off my skin-test charts.*

## 2013: wild welts, everything lights up
My first full panel lit up almost across the board — trees, grasses, weeds, molds, cat, dog. If
it grew, it got me. That's the baseline: a back full of welts, reacting to nearly the whole tray.

> `[drop pic here: docs/story/assets/2013-baseline-sheet — the handwritten "wild welts" battery]`

I started allergy shots (immunotherapy) and stayed on them.

## ~13 years on shots (2013 → 2026): almost everything quiets
Immunotherapy is slow — you climb for months, then hold for years. Over more than a decade,
almost everything fell off my panel. You can watch it happen between two later tests:

| Allergen | 2021 (still on shots) | 2023 (still on shots) |
|---|---|---|
| **Trees** (oak, cottonwood, boxelder, mulberry, birch, elm) | several **positive** (intradermal) | **essentially all negative** |
| **Ragweed** | **positive** (10/35) | **negative** |
| **Molds** (cladosporium, aspergillus, rhizopus…) | several **positive** | **negative** |
| **Dog** | **positive** (8/8) | **negative** |
| **Grass** (Bermuda, Bahia, Johnson, rye, KORT) | strong positive | **still strong** — Bermuda 11/47, Bahia 15, Johnson 15/44, rye 11 |
| **Cat** | positive (6/12) | **still positive** (5/12) — never quit |
| **Dust mites** | — | positive (D. farinae 6/8, D. pteronyssinus 5/10) |

> `[drop pic here: docs/story/assets/2021-grids — the green/blue/yellow color-coded panels]`
> `[drop pic here: docs/story/assets/welts-to-dots — the progression photos, wild welts → quiet dots]`

**The trees, the ragweed, the molds, the dog — the shots knocked them out. Grass barely budged.**
That's not a memory; that's my chart. Grass is the one that wouldn't fully let go.

## 2026: I had to stop
This year money got tight — single dad, a little one with real medical needs — and I stopped the
shots. Here's the part nobody warns you about: **you can't just pause.** Stop long enough and it
resets. To get the benefit back I'd have to build up from the bottom again, months of climbing,
and I can't afford that right now. `[FILL: month I stopped / what my allergist said about restarting]`

So the thing that worked is a thing I lost access to — at least for now. Which is exactly why a
map that helps me pick my *air* is worth building. When the medical lever is out of reach,
**where you live is the lever you've got left.**

## The honest takeaway
No place would have cured me — grass survived 13 years of shots aimed straight at it. But some
places tax me far less than others, and I can plan around a season, choose my landscaping, and
weight a move toward my easier air. That's the whole tool: not a cure, a plan.
