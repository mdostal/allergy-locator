# Why this exists

You already paid for a test that can help you pick a city. Almost nobody uses it that way.

When you move, you price the house, the schools, the commute, the cost of a dinner out. You do
not price the **air** — whether the place you're moving to happens to be built out of the exact
plants your immune system picks a fight with. And most of us are carrying the answer around
already, in a lab result we glanced at once and filed.

## The gap no tool fills
Every allergen product on the market answers *"what's the pollen near me, right now?"* Useful if
you already live somewhere and want to know whether to take a Zyrtec today. Useless for the
bigger decision: **where should I live?** That's the reverse lookup — start from *your*
allergens, rank the whole map by how much each place will personally cost you — and nothing does
it. So this does.

## Why it matters more than it sounds
Because the medical lever isn't always available. The person who built this spent ~13 years on
allergy shots that worked — and then had to stop when money got tight with a young kid to raise
(see [`MY-STORY.md`](./MY-STORY.md)). Immunotherapy is real and effective, but it costs money and
years of consistency, and life can take it away.

**Geography is a lever you don't pay a clinic for every month.** You can't change your immune
system for free — but you can know, before you sign a mortgage, whether you're moving into your
triggers or away from them. That knowledge is sitting in a panel you already own. This tool just
reads it against the map.

## What it is (and isn't)
- **Is:** an honest, personal relocation filter. Pick your allergens, see your best/worst air,
  get the levers you actually control.
- **Isn't:** a promise that any city cures you (it can't), a medical device, or a data-harvesting
  app. It's open, free, and keeps your profile in your browser.
