# Care access — methodology

Dimension 2 of `docs/ROADMAP.md`'s Livability Atlas (allergy is dimension 1). This is the
first slice of v5's "second real dataset" work — building a genuine second dataset before
attempting to generalize the map engine beyond allergy, rather than guessing at an
abstraction with only one real case to design against.

## What this measures

For each of the 168 spine cities (`data/cities.json`), the nearest facility and estimated
drive time across 3 layers:
- **`pediatric_cardiac`** — congenital heart surgery programs (64 facilities).
- **`pediatric_specialty`** — broader pediatric subspecialty children's hospitals (92
  facilities).
- **`general`** — nearest adequate acute-care / major medical center, for everyone, not
  just pediatric needs (168 facilities — effectively one within reach of every spine city).

## Method

`est_drive_min = haversine_distance_mi * 1.25 road-factor / 55 mph`, converted to minutes.
Distance is straight-line (haversine great-circle), **not real routing** — a documented,
honest approximation consistent with every other estimate in this project, not a claimed
precise travel time. Tiers: `<=30` / `<=60` / `<=120` / `120+` minutes.

Generated by `scripts/gen_care_access.py` from the three hospital-facility lists
(`data/hospitals-general.json`, `data/hospitals-pediatric-cardiac.json`,
`data/hospitals-pediatric-specialty.json`) against the city spine. Re-run it any time those
source lists change — the output is fully reproducible, not hand-maintained.

## Known limitations (shown, not smoothed over)

- **Straight-line distance underestimates real drive time** in mountainous/rural terrain
  (e.g. Rocky Mountain West) where roads don't go directly — the 1.25 road-factor is a
  single national fudge factor, not terrain-aware.
- **The hospital lists themselves have no single authoritative source documented** — they
  were compiled in an earlier planning pass without a formally cited data source per
  facility (unlike allergy's USDA/GBIF/NOAA sourcing). Treat them as a good-faith, editable
  starting list, not a verified-complete directory.
- **A real gap was found and fixed before this methodology doc existed**: 9 cities
  (including New York City, Baltimore, and St. Louis) had a verified pediatric
  cardiac-surgery program in the cardiac list but ZERO entry in the pediatric-specialty
  list — so their "nearest specialty care" silently resolved to a much farther facility
  than reality (New York City resolved to Philadelphia, 111 minutes, before the fix). Those
  hospitals were added to the specialty list, reusing the exact name/city/state/lat/lon
  already verified in the cardiac list. This is the KIND of gap that likely still exists
  elsewhere in these lists (a real hospital simply not yet added) — `tests/care-access-data
  .test.ts` pins the specific cities found so far as a regression check, but does not prove
  the lists are now complete.
- **Nearest-facility only, not "reachable for planned care."** Like the allergy tool's own
  documented soft spots, this doesn't yet model that an established care team often stays
  the anchor for planned/ongoing care even after a move (see `docs/ROADMAP.md` dimension 2)
  — a future refinement, not attempted here.
